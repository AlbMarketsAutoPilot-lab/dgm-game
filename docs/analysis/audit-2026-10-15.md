# Full audit, 15 Oct 2026 (owner's playtest list)

> **Update, same day: the owner's real-life tests were right, and the rules were changed (GCD R2, R17, R20).**
> "6 to enter, then 2" moved the token to square 6 when the 6 came on the third throw at Home: rule R17 (stair steps,
> and a 6 on the 3rd throw counting as a moving 6). Fixed by the owner's rules: a 6 only lets you in, then 6, 2 →
> square 2 always; no stair steps, so two players never share square 1; every 6 gives exactly one more throw
> (6, 6, 1 → 13, the turn ends); the Shortcut is removed. All of these are now tests on the real boards
> (`test/boards.test.ts`, `test/rules.test.ts`). Measured (40,000 games per board): every seat wins 24.7–25.2%,
> long 52.1 rounds, short 30.1; lead changes 8.0 / 6.8, close finishes 10.3% / 18.6%. The boards were resized so
> the spiral fills them without the Shortcuts. The postcard "Shortcut Jackpot" became "Dodged It!" (Bonus card).
> Sections 1 and 3 below are the first report, kept as written.

What was checked: the rules engine (scripted throws on the real boards, plus 20,000 random games per board with
checks after every turn), the game screen in a real browser (whole games with 1 and 2 people, with a check after
every turn that each drawn token stands where the engine says), the board drawing (every square against every
other), 10 screen sizes, and speed on a normal and a 6× slower processor.

## 3. Measurements for the owner's questions (40,000 games per row)

### 3.1 Two players on square 1 (R17 stair steps)

| Board / option | seat spread (pts) | rounds | bumps per game |
|---|---|---|---|
| long, today | 0.44 | 52.1 | 7.46 |
| long, a stair step can be bumped | 0.41 | 52.0 | 7.75 |
| long, **no stair steps** (3rd miss: move normally, still lose a lantern) | 0.42 | 51.7 | 7.81 |
| short, today | 0.63 | 31.0 | 6.96 |
| short, a stair step can be bumped | 0.49 | 30.9 | 7.25 |
| short, **no stair steps** | 0.44 | 30.5 | 7.12 |

All fair. **Recommendation: no stair steps.** Two players never share a square, and nobody "moves but stays on
square 1" any more (the main source of the confusion).

### 3.2 The Shortcut (R20)

"Always-Shortcut" = one player who always takes the Shortcut against three who never do (fair = 25%).

| Variant | always-Shortcut wins |
|---|---|
| long today (13 / 19 stones, a jackpot and a Bog stone each) | 25.3% |
| long, half length (6 / 9 stones) | 26.6% |
| long, **3 / 4 stones, one Bog stone, no jackpot** | **26.0%** |
| long, 4 / 5 stones, a jackpot and a Bog stone | 26.4% |
| long, no Shortcut | – (game 51.2 rounds instead of 52.1) |
| short today (6 / 9 stones) | 24.8% |
| short, 3 / 4 stones, jackpot + Bog | 26.8% |
| short, **2 / 3 stones, one Bog stone** | **25.4%** |
| short, no Shortcut | – (29.8 rounds instead of 31.0) |

**Recommendation:** keep the crossroads, but make the Shortcut very short with one Bog stone and no jackpot: a clear
choice ("save 10 squares, or risk the Bog"), still fair (26.0% / 25.4%).

### 3.3 Good and bad squares

| | good : bad squares | good share of landings | players with 3+ bad in a row | regions all-good or all-bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|

A second Bonus card square (owner's idea), measured:

| | rounds | lead changes | late overtakes | close finishes | falls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| long today | 52.1 | 7.6 | 1.90 | 10.4% | 4.61 |
| long + Bonus card on 155 | 50.6 | 7.3 | 1.82 | 11.0% | 4.22 |

| short + Bonus card on 85 | 29.2 | 6.3 | 2.39 | 21.4% | 5.03 |

More positive feeling (fewer falls actually felt, closer finishes), a little less back-and-forth (fewer lead
changes). The bunching is the bigger issue: a bad end is the paper game's signature ("Forgot your keys AGAIN"),
but whole regions that are always good or always bad are predictable. **Recommendation:** first the second Bonus
card, then (separate step) re-order squares so every region has both good and bad, keeping the signature events.
