# Full audit before phase G (27 Nov 2026)

> Owner's request (27 Nov 2026): a full audit of "Don't Get Mad!" before the release (phase G), from `main` at the
> merge of phase F (`0db276f`). Audit only: no fix is made until the owner approves it, one by one, on a new branch
> from `main`. Three parts: **B** the code (technical), **C** the logic in real games, **D** the design at all 22
> screen sizes of `tools/browser/screens.mjs`. Out of scope (owner): the Android and Windows wrappers (`android/`,
> `windows/`) and `public/`. Outdated documents count as findings.

## Part B: the code

### What was checked

- Every source file in `src/` read line by line (engine, game screen, Mr. Lin's panel, popups, notifications, tips,
  his words at "Pass the die", Records, win clock, saved games, postcards, How to play, start screen, sound, board and
  stage drawing, `app.css`), the board file, the tests and the browser checks, with the history of Steps 3–9, H and I
  (phases A–F) in `docs/`.
- `npm run typecheck`: no error. `npm test`: 198 of 198 pass.
- 100,000 games with random players (people and robots mixed, 2–4 players, every choice answered at random):
  9.5 million turns, a check after every turn. **No rule broken** (no two tokens on one square, no negative cards,
  Home / tree / boat flags only where they belong, the winner on the last square, no game stopped by the round limit).
  The screen's own record of where each token stands and how many cards each player holds, rebuilt from the engine's
  events, **matched the engine after every turn**.
- The app's engine against the measured simulator for players who use their card (the existing test covers robots
  only): 900 games, **identical** throw for throw.
- Text from data files: every piece goes through `esc()`; button labels are plain text. No problem found.
- Storage (saved game, postcards, Records, tips, sound, guide): every read and write is guarded; blocked or tampered
  storage gives no error (also covered by the tests).
- `npm run test:browser` (full run): see the end of this part.

## Part C: the logic in real games

### What was played

Real games through the real screen in Chromium, tapping the buttons as a player would, with every notification,
Mr. Lin panel, stamp, area sign, button, turn line and avatar tag written down in order (a scratch script, not in the
repository). Choices at random (card used or kept, Swap Market partner).

- Fast mode, PC size: 1 person + 3 robots, 2 people + 2 robots, 4 people, 2 people (whole games, 3,700 lines).
- Normal speed: 1 person + 1 robot, three whole games (phone 390 × 844 and PC 1366 × 768), the person taking about
  2.5 seconds to read each panel, with a check of whether each stamp and area sign can be seen.
- Ten chosen moments, set up from a saved game, each at phone and PC size: the card question at the fallen tree, the
  card question in the Forest, taking a card at the Swap Market (3 to choose from), the 2-player Swap Market, winning
  with the Dragon Boat's 4, Plot Twist with nobody ahead, a 6 at Skipping Stones, stuck at the tree with the backward
  move waiting, a doubled roll near the end, the die faces with a backward move waiting.
- Seen working as agreed: Home (question every turn with a card, the Home story only the first time), free to go,
  bumps and "back Home: no 6 needed", every area effect with and without a card, the Dragon Boat (waiting, missing,
  winning), the Swap Market (give, take, automatic with 2 players, nobody has a card), Plot Twist (also with nobody
  ahead: only the notification), squares 7, 61, 72, 96, 99, 100, the finish and the win popup ("{p} made it!", the
  finish story only for a person, the Records line, the new postcards), "Welcome back" for a continued game, Mr. Lin
  never speaking in a robot's turn (only his welcome), every tip once, at the agreed moment. **No console error** in any
  game.
