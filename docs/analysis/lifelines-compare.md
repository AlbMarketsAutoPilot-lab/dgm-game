All versions: frozen luck rules + waiting stairs, roadblock, two roads; 3 lifelines at the start; at 0 the player plays on ("Attention! You have no lifelines left…"), the 4th loss = out; no upper limit.

- **A:** −1 no 6 at Start/trap · +1 three 6s in a row
- **B:** −1 no 6 at Start/trap · +1 a 6 that gets you out · −1 sent back to Start (captured or square 10)
- **B2:** same as B, but only captures cost a lifeline (square 10 does not)

"Short game" = fastest 25% of games, "long game" = slowest 25% (in rounds).
