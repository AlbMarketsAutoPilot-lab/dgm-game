## 4. How the two roads were tuned

The first try used shorter wild roads with a jackpot and a fall of the same size: the wild road won 29–34%. The reason is the paper board's catch-up squares. A fall puts you back before square 75 (swap with the leader) or 138 (Snake Leader), so on this board a fall costs much less than a jackpot of the same size gives. Fair versions found (always-wild player, 4 × 40,000 games):

| Wild road | Jackpot | Fall | Always-wild player wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shorter (7 / 10 squares) | jump to the end of the wild road | into the trap (30) | 25.6% |
| Same length (13 / 19 squares) | 5 squares past the merge (→ 100 / 185) | into the trap (30) | 25.2% → **chosen** (bigger jackpot) |
