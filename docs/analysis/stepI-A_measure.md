## What it shows

- **Fair:** every seat within ±0.4 of a fair share with 2, 3 and 4 players. No game ran out of rounds.
- **Exciting:** more lead changes and late overtakes than both earlier boards, the same close finishes as the v2
  short board (twice the paper's). The Dragon Boat decides 13% of games (0.9 rides per game, 3 in 4 miss the 4).
- **Fewer jackpots (4.6 per game, paper 6.7, v2 short 7.5):** the only target missed. It comes from the 20-square
  limit on event moves; the area effects (gifted 6, swaps) move players a lot too but are not counted as jackpots.
- **Waiting is fine:** 2.1% of players wait 10+ turns without moving (2 robots: 3.5%). At Home nobody waits 10+ turns
  (robots average 1.5 turns there: the forced card on the 3rd throw). **The tree is now the main wait:** 1.2 players
  per game get stuck there, 0.85 turns waiting each on average, and 1.8% of players wait 10+ turns there (a robot
  with no card left must roll the 6).
- **Strategy counts almost nothing:** the robot wins 25.4% among 3 simple players (fair 25%, ±0.27), the simple player
  24.7% among 3 robots. Using the card at Home at once or keeping it for a punishment is worth about the same. (v2:
  the Normal robot won 31.9% on the short board, because lanterns were a strong choice.)
- **Cards flow:** per game (4 robots) 2.9 cards won on 30 / 45 / 85, 1.5 from the Seaside, 0.6 moved at the Swap
  Market; used: 2.4 forced at Home / the tree, 3.4 on red squares, 1.4 on area punishments.
- **Sixes:** per game, 0.41 runs of exactly three 6s, 0.07 of four, 0.01 of five (the sixes postcards).
