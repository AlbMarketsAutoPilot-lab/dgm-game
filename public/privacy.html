Privacy Policy

Don't Get Mad! (web game and Android app) · Connect AL · Effective 15 October 2026

In short: Don't Get Mad! collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Don't Get Mad! is made and published by Connect AL ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game, in the web version and in the Android app.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on our servers, sell or share any personal or sensitive information. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social media or tracking code. The game does not use cookies.

3. What the game keeps on your device

To work, the game saves a few things in your device's own storage (the app's storage on Android, or the browser's local storage for the web version). This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

WhatWhy
The game in progress: the board, the number of players, which seats are people or the computer, and the dice sequence and choices of the current gameSo you can continue a game where you left it
The postcards collected in the postcard albumTo show your collection
Whether sound effects or music have been mutedTo remember your choice
Whether the "How to play" guide has been seenSo it is shown only the first time

Player "names" in the game are the token colours (Blue, Red, Green, Yellow); you cannot type in personal names. The game also stores its own files on your device (the offline copy of the game) so that it works without an internet connection.

4. Internet connection

The Android app contains all of its files and does not need an internet connection to play. The game sends no information about you or your play to anyone.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser downloads the game, GitHub, as the host, receives the technical information every website receives (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page; this is handled under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive this information. The Android app is downloaded through Google Play, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Device features and permissions

The game asks for no special permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or photos. When the sound switch is on, the game can make the phone vibrate briefly at exciting moments (for example a 6 or a win); this uses no personal information and is off when sound is off.

6. Children

Don't Get Mad! is a family game suitable for children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It shows no advertising and has no chat, links to social media, or in-app purchases.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

The information listed in section 3 stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it: on Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Don't Get Mad! → Storage → Clear storage); in a web browser, clear the site data for this website. Starting a new game also replaces the saved game. Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

The game loads only its own files and blocks content from other websites. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, none can be exposed by us.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the game ever started to collect any information, this policy would be updated before that change is released, and the game would ask for your consent where the law requires it.

10. Contact

For any question about this policy or your privacy, contact Connect AL through the game's project page: github.com/AlbMarketsAutoPilot-lab/dgm-game/issues.
